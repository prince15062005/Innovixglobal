Common Questions

Quick answers to help you get started

Do you provide custom finish requests?
Yes, we discuss finish, size, and project-based specifications with our sales and operations teams. Contact us with your requirements for a custom consultation.
Can you support bulk or dealer orders?
We supply architectural and dealer channels with project coordination for volume requirements. Reach out to our sales team for dealer partnership opportunities.
Are your products suitable for hospitality and commercial use?
Yes. Our hardware is chosen for durability, finish consistency, and premium everyday performance in hotels, offices, retail spaces, and corporate reception areas.
What is the lead time for orders?
Lead times vary based on product availability, finish selection, and order quantity. Standard items typically ship within 2-3 weeks. Custom finishes may require 4-6 weeks. Our team will provide exact timelines during quotation.
Do you ship internationally?
Currently we focus on Pan-India delivery with our secure logistics network. For international enquiries, please contact our sales team to discuss feasibility and shipping arrangements.
