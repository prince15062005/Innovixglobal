ABOUT US

The Structure of Style

INNOVIX GLOBAL stands at the forefront of architectural hardware innovation, creating premium door handles and mortise handles that define luxury living spaces.

BRAND PHILOSOPHY

Our Brand Philosophy

At INNOVIX GLOBAL, we believe that architectural hardware is more than just functional components—it's the jewelry of architecture. Every handle, every mortise lock, every finish tells a story of craftsmanship and design excellence.

Our journey began with a simple vision: to create architectural hardware that elevates spaces from ordinary to extraordinary. Today, that vision drives every product we design, every finish we perfect, and every detail we refine.

We specialize in two core categories—Main Door Handles and Mortise Handles—each engineered with precision using premium materials including brass, stainless steel, and specialized alloys. Our finishes range from classic gold and rose gold to contemporary black, graphite, and PVD coatings, ensuring the perfect match for any architectural style.

SPECIALIZATION

Architectural Hardware Expertise

Focused mastery in premium door hardware categories

Main Door Handles

Our main door handles collection features 19 distinct designs (IGPH-211 through IGPH-337), each available in multiple premium finishes and sizes ranging from 200mm to 1200mm. Materials include SS + Brass combinations, Full Brass, SS + Aluminum, and Brass + Wood for natural warmth.

  • 19 unique designs
  • Multiple finish options
  • Sizes: 200-1200mm
  • Premium material combinations

Mortise Handles

Our mortise handles collection features 22 sophisticated designs (IGM-001 through IGM-022), all crafted from Full Brass with Rose sizing. Finishes include SS, Graphite Black, AB, Z.Black, and premium PVD coatings in Gold, Rose Gold, and Copper variations.

  • 22 unique designs
  • Full Brass construction
  • PVD premium finishes
  • Rose size standard
