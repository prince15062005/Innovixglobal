About Innovix Global

Building the future of luxury door hardware through metallurgical precision, modern aesthetics, and uncompromising durability.

Our Heritage & Vision

Pioneering Architectural
Hardware Excellence

Innovix Global was established with a singular mission: to design and engineer world-class architectural door handles and security locksets under the flagship brand Luxurin.

Based in Rajkot, Gujarat — India's renowned heartland of precision engineering and metal casting — our facility combines traditional hand-carved toolmaking with advanced computerized finishing technology.

Vision

To become the premier architectural hardware brand trusted by top architects across India and globally.

Quality Promise

Zero compromise on raw metal purity, salt spray resistance, and mechanical tension life.
