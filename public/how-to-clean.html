PRODUCT CARE

How to Clean

Proper care ensures your INNOVIX GLOBAL architectural hardware maintains its premium appearance and functionality for years to come.

RECOMMENDED

Do Use

The only recommended cleaning method for all our products

Use Cotton Cloth to Clean

Always use a soft, clean cotton cloth to gently wipe the surface of your hardware products. A microfiber cloth is ideal as it won't leave lint or scratch the finish. For routine maintenance, simply dust with a dry cotton cloth weekly to prevent buildup.

STRICTLY AVOID

Do Not Use

These products and substances will permanently damage your hardware finishes

Thinner

Chemical thinners can strip protective coatings and damage the finish permanently, causing discoloration and corrosion.

Masking Tape

Adhesive residue from tapes can react with finishes and cause discoloration that cannot be reversed.

Acid

Acidic cleaners will etch and corrode metal surfaces, destroying the premium finish and compromising structural integrity.

Toilet Cleaner

Harsh chemicals in toilet cleaners are too aggressive for architectural hardware and will cause irreversible damage.

Detergent Powder

Abrasive particles in detergent powders can scratch and dull the surface finish, leaving permanent marks.

Important Notice

If you do not follow above instructions Company will not be Responsible for the Product Damage.

MAINTENANCE TIPS

Long-term Care

Additional guidelines to preserve the beauty of your hardware

Regular Dusting

Gently dust with a dry cotton cloth weekly to prevent buildup of dirt and grime.

Dry Immediately

If exposed to moisture or water, dry immediately with a soft cloth to prevent water spots.

Handle with Care

Avoid impacts and scratches from rings, keys, or tools when operating doors.

Soft Cloth Only

Never use abrasive pads, steel wool, scouring pads, or rough materials on the finish.
